Credit Risk Modelling

  • Using 466,285 loans, built a probability-of-default (PD) model with logistic regression, turned it into a 300–850 credit scorecard, and added loss-given-default (LGD) and exposure-at-default (EAD) models.
  • On 93,257 loans held out from training, the PD model reached an AUC of 0.684 (Gini 0.368), and its predicted default rates were close to the actual rates in all 10 risk groups. A cut-off score of 600 would approve 74% of these loans and lower their bad rate from 10.9% to 7.8%.
  • Combined PD × LGD × EAD into an expected loss of $509 million on $6.66 billion lent (7.6%), rising from 2.1% for grade A to 20.5% for grade G.
